Whenever their will any side effect on our page :
useEffect() 
It is a hook, which is used to handle any side-effect on our UI.

useEffect have 3 cases:
Case 1 : Without any Dependency
    In this case it will re-render on any update in a webpage.

Case 2 : With Empty Array
    In this it will only render at the first refresh after that their will be no re-renders.

Case 3 : With Dependency
    It will only trigger when their is any change in our dependency.

Syntax:
useEffect(callback,dependency)
